02 · Production · Sidecoach Sports

An AI gateway and agents a youth sports company can trust

The company wanted LLM features and AI operations, under COPPA and FERPA, with no platform team. Every team needed its own access and spend limits, no student data could reach a model, and every agent action had to be traceable.

  • AI gateway: Entra ID sign-in, per-team API keys, PII redaction and OpenTelemetry tracing in front of every LLM call.
  • MCP agent service with RAG over internal content.
  • SRE agent suite on Azure AI Foundry that triages alerts and opens Jira tickets, with a human approval before any runbook runs.

03 · Production · Zycus, enterprise SaaS

Moving 2,000 servers onto Kubernetes, then bringing them back after ransomware

Zycus runs procurement software for global enterprises across 7 environments, 4 of them production. I led the move from VMs to 50+ Kubernetes clusters with GitOps, and led a team of 5 that standardized how everything was provisioned.

Then ransomware took 1,500+ servers and 80 to 90 services per environment offline. As a first responder, I helped restore the fleet in 7 days, then re-architected the network and ran 4 months of hardening.
