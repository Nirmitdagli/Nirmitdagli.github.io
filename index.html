            GPU performance · open source
          

          
LLM Serving Bench and Fused GPU Kernels

          

            
The hardware context
NVIDIA Tesla T4 (16 GB, 320 GB/s). Qwen2.5-1.5B in fp16 and AWQ int4, 512 input and 128 output tokens, identical memory budget per engine.

            
The memory constraint
Decode is memory-bandwidth bound: every step reads all weights and the KV cache. Softmax, RMSNorm and SwiGLU move far more bytes than they compute.

            
My core low-level contribution
An async load generator that times every streamed token and sweeps 1 to 64 concurrent users across vLLM and SGLang, plus fused Triton kernels (forward and backward) and a tiled GEMM checked against cuBLAS.

          

          

            
AWQ int4 cut decode 2.3x (20.0 → 8.5 ms/token) · fused kernels reach 78% of T4 peak bandwidth

Multi-Cloud Kubernetes Platform for Enterprise SaaS

          

            
The context
Enterprise procurement SaaS for global customers, across 7 environments (4 production) on AWS, Azure, GCP, Rancher and VMware.

            
The scale constraint
2,000+ servers to move onto Kubernetes without downtime, then a ransomware attack that took 1,500+ servers and 80 to 90 services per environment offline.

            
My core technical contribution
Led the migration into 50+ clusters (EKS, AKS, GKE, Rancher, OpenShift) with ArgoCD GitOps, Kyverno policies and Velero backups. Led a team of 5 standardizing Terraform, Ansible and Jenkins provisioning. First responder in the recovery: restored the fleet, re-architected the network (Transit Gateway, segmented VPCs, 20 Palo Alto firewalls) and ran 4 months of hardening.
