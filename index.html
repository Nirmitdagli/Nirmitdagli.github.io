Open source 78% llm-kernels Fused Triton kernels reaching 70 to 78% of the T4's peak memory bandwidth. Details

Fused softmax, RMSNorm and SwiGLU (forward and backward), plus a tiled GEMM compared against cuBLAS. The kernels run at 223 to 250 GB/s and are 4.9 to 10.1x faster than the PyTorch eager reference.

It is the inside view of what the race shows: decode is memory-bound.
